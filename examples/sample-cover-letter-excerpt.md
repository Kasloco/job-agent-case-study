# Sample Cover Letter Excerpt (fictional)

Written for the fictional lead in [sample-lead-row.md](sample-lead-row.md). The employer is invented. The candidate lines are drawn from a real background profile, which is the only source letters may use. The writing rules forbid inventing tools, titles, or results.

---

> Brightwater is past the "should we use AI" stage. The posting describes licenses rolled out, a few early adopters, and an ops team that hasn't changed how it works yet. That gap between access and adoption is the work I want to do.
>
> In my current role I co-wrote my company's AI integration strategy and then helped carry it out: choosing the first use cases, building working prototypes, and showing non-technical colleagues how to use them on real tasks. I've learned that adoption comes from one workflow that saves a team an hour a day, not from a training deck.
>
> For your first 90 days I'd start with a short workflow review across ops and finance, pick two or three high-volume tasks to pilot, stand up a small champions group, and report usage and time saved each month to the VP of Operations Excellence.

---

**Rules this excerpt follows**

- Opens with the employer's situation, not the candidate.
- One or two concrete proofs, not a résumé recap.
- A specific plan that matches the posting.
- No buzzwords, no claims about tools or results the candidate can't back up.
